# Rules for AI agents working on this guide

Read [`GUIDE-PRINCIPLES.md`](GUIDE-PRINCIPLES.md) (shared by all Matrix guides), then [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md)
(this guide only), before you change anything. The short version:

1. **Do not invent content.** Driving, safety and equipment rules come from the owner and the manager. If something
   is unclear or looks wrong, ask. Rejected ideas are marked **(rejected)** in DESIGN.md: do not bring them back.
2. **General rules only.** No dates, times, places, routes or names: the guide must work for any future job.
3. **The four key points** (DESIGN.md 9.1) keep their words, icons and order unless the owner decides otherwise.
4. **Say each thing once, and at most 3 bullets per card.**
5. **Pictures are inline SVG built from the helpers in the script.** Same palette, same flat style, true proportions,
   right-hand drive. No image files, icon fonts or libraries.
6. **Stay inside the task.** Explain the problem, how the page works now, and what your change alters, before you
   change it.
7. **Shared rules live in GUIDE-PRINCIPLES.md**, and the same file is in every guide repository. If you change a
   shared rule, change it in all of them in the same piece of work.
8. **Report honestly.** Say what you tested, what you only reasoned about, and what you could not test. Use the
   checklists in DESIGN.md and GUIDE-PRINCIPLES.md.
9. **Keep the docs true.** If you change structure, behaviour or a rule, update DESIGN.md in the same commit.
